# The AI-Agnostic Principle Manifesto

Software has always separated products from tools.

We do not commit:

- IDE preferences
- Keyboard layouts
- Personal terminal settings

AI assistants are the next evolution of development tools.

They should receive the same separation.

---

The repository is the shared reality.

The AI workflow is personal creativity.

Different developers should bring different intelligence to the same codebase.

A project should not depend on the mind of one agent.

It should survive the evolution of all agents.

---

The future of software development belongs to teams where humans and AI collaborate freely without locking the project into one artificial mind.
